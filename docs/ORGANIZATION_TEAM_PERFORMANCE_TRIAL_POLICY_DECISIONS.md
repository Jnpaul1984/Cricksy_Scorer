The five and three are fixed owner decisions. Readiness is evidence only; it does not grant access, start, consume, or reset a trial (#628).

Current code confirms the missing foundation:

- `backend/services/school_team_readiness_service.py` selects five prior games using an exclusive trusted played-date boundary, original 25-over provenance, strict both-role recorded-ledger validation, same-date ambiguity rejection, and 250-game/5 MiB bounds. Its internal selected sources include Game ID, metadata revision, and ledger digest; its response excludes those identifiers. It recalculates after corrections. It is a *prior-match* selector, not a consumption ledger.
- `backend/services/organization_team_performance_access.py` and `backend/sql_app/models.py::OrganizationTeamPerformanceGrant` implement an independent, default-off, **organization-scoped** operator grant. Every private read checks active user, organization, membership, contextual owner/admin/coach role, current grant validity, and exact Team ownership. The grant has operator/reason/validity/revocation fields, but no trial identity or allowance. Its organization scope does not decide trial scope.
- `backend/services/school_match_service.py` captures original overs and frozen Team attribution at setup. `backend/services/game_performance_metadata_service.py` supports audited, revision-checked date/format correction. `backend/routes/gameplay.py` can finalize a game and undo a terminal delivery, reopening a completed Game. These facts make “consume on completed status” and correction policy material business decisions, not settled implementation details.
- `backend/services/school_team_report_service.py` and the private report route compose authorized, read-only evidence. They do not consume an allowance. Searches for `team_performance_trial`, `TeamPerformanceTrial`, and trial-consumption symbols in `backend/`, `frontend/`, and `docs/` found no Team Performance trial state or ledger. Existing personal subscription/trial concepts do not supply organization policy.

## Approved lifecycle specification — implementation pending

`not_started` is absence of a trial row. Readiness `not_ready | ready | data_unavailable` is derived from bounded evidence and never creates trial state. An active contextual owner/admin may activate only while the independent Cricksy-controlled trial availability is on. Durable trial state distinguishes `active`, `exhausted`, `expired`, `revoked`, and `review_required`; the underlying immutable event history remains even after expiry or revocation. The 90-day deadline is anchored at activation and keeps advancing during review. A Cricksy operator may extend it only for a verified system fault with an explicit audit event. An inactive organization, archived Team, removed membership, disallowed role or effective revocation denies access at every request, including retained reports.

| Transition | Approved preconditions | Atomic outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `not_started → active` | Active contextual owner/admin explicitly requests activation with a confirmed valid IANA venue timezone; default-off trial availability is enabled; active organization and exact nonarchived Team; five-match readiness `ready` at the server-derived local activation date; no prior Team trial. | Freeze five prerequisite source identities/revisions/digests; record scope, actor, reason, UTC activation time, timezone, local date, 90-day server-time deadline, policy version, allowance `3`, and first audit event. No Game consumed. |
| `active → active` (one consumption) | Trusted server-side completion event for a distinct post-activation official Game; exact Team/original 25-over format, trusted date and valid ledger; verified order; current access and state; fewer than three **valid** consumed identities. | Insert immutable Game consumption identity and audit event; derived count increases by one. Duplicate event returns its existing outcome. A failed trial processor cannot fail Free completion. |
| `active → exhausted` | Exactly the third valid qualifying Game commits before the deadline. | In the same controlled transition, retain an immutable tenant-scoped report snapshot computed from rolling verified Games 4–8, including Game 3 with provenance/revision fingerprints, and record completion time and 14-day read-only deadline. Never use the exclusive prior-match selector to omit Game 3. |
| `active/exhausted → review_required` | A correction, undo, date/format change, ledger change or attribution conflict disputes a frozen prerequisite or consumed Game. | Preserve identities and audit history, flag affected evidence as unverified, withhold the invalidated report view, and block further consumption pending authorized review. The 90-day clock continues. |
| `review_required → active/exhausted` | Explicitly authorized Cricksy operator verifies the evidence and records a reasoned resolution. | Keep valid consumption or append a compensating reversal for verified invalid consumption; restore at most the allowance needed for three valid Games. A replacement Game must qualify independently. Never delete the original claim. |
| `active/review_required → expired` | The 90-day deadline passes, including while under review, unless a previously audited system-fault extension applies. | Deny new consumption; retain ledger and audit events. No automatic extension or reset. |
| `active/exhausted/review_required → revoked` | Effective Cricksy-controlled trial revocation. | Deny trial access and new consumption while preserving immutable history and independent pilot-grant state. |

No transition is triggered by a page load, a readiness/report read, an API retry, or a Free scoring request merely because the Game exists. No historical Game is silently backfilled into a trial. An excluded or same-date ambiguous candidate is held for review, not assumed charged or replaced. Staff status may expose only state, `0–3` valid-used/remaining counts, and applicable expiry and retained-report dates; no Game IDs, child/player identity, raw deliveries, audit actor IDs, or notes. School and Club receive identical rules.

## Approved transaction and idempotency contract

The migration must give the trial a unique `(organization_id, team_id)` key and explicit policy version. A separate append-only event/consumption ledger needs a unique `(trial_id, game_id)` claim identity plus an idempotency key for the trusted completion event. A compensating reversal references the original claim and never deletes it. PostgreSQL constraints are the final defense, beyond preflight counts. Activation re-evaluates bounded five-match readiness. The server-side completion processor checks current organization, Team and availability; locks the trial row; verifies **frozen** prerequisites without substituting newer matches; validates the candidate Game and immutable event order under a consistent transaction view; and inserts claim, event, rolling report and state change atomically. Two simultaneous completions serialize; at most three distinct **valid** qualifying Games commit. Duplicate events return the same outcome without extra charge. Trial processing must be independently recoverable and never abort Free scoring or match completion. Stale revision, changed prerequisite, unavailable evidence, exceeded workload bound or lost access fails closed before trial mutation. Staff responses must be non-disclosing on foreign scope.

The five prior Game fingerprints are internal evidence only. Post-activation Game identity comes from frozen saved-Team attribution and trusted original format, never current `overs_limit`, fixture schedule, creation time or player data. Same-day activation or later-confirmed historical evidence requires review unless immutable server-side completion evidence proves order. At each valid premium Game, the rolling five-match calculation uses exactly the newest verified cohort, never an unverified substitute. At the third Game, the persisted snapshot includes Game 3's checked evidence. Snapshots remain immutable; invalidation is a separate audited state/evidence event and must suppress stale “verified” presentation. Read-only access ends 14 days after completion and is reauthorized on every request. A clean downgrade may remove an empty schema; a downgrade with activation or audit history must refuse rather than erase it. No billing or future-paid table is part of this foundation.

## Threat analysis and implementation plan

| Risk | Required control/test |
|---|---|
| Cross-tenant Team/Game or another organization's opponent | Exact organization and Team predicates in the service and DB keys; direct-service and route denial with no foreign identifiers/counts. |
| Trial availability, grant, membership, role, or organization changes after activation | Revalidate at each access/transition; keep pilot and trial authority separate; distinguish 403/404 per existing pilot contract; revoke/expire/inactivate tests for School and Club. |
| Duplicate, reordered, or concurrent events | Trusted completion event, unique Game identity, idempotency key, row lock/transaction, real PostgreSQL two-writer test, and a fourth-Game rejection without blocking Free scoring. |
| Edited date, format, ledger, or undo after a charge | Revision/digest check, immutable correction audit, Cricksy-only resolution and compensating reversal for verified invalid claims; withhold invalidated report evidence. |
| Same-day chronology or historical import | T4 order evidence or review-required suppression; no Game-ID/time inference; no retroactive consumption. |
| Trial bypass through report/status reads | Read-only typed allowlist; no consumption on GET, retries, stale UI responses, or Free scoring paths. |
| Workload or migration rollback | Preserve 250-game/5 MiB readiness bounds; explicit over-limit result; clean upgrade/downgrade/re-upgrade and refusal to erase nonempty ledger history. |
