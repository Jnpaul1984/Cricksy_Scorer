# Organization Team Performance Staff Pilot — Locked Scope

This document records the delivered aggregate pilot from issue #626 and locks the additive, bounded history/readiness foundation for issue #628. It authorizes a private, read-only School/Club Team Performance pilot surface only. It does not authorize enabling access for any real organization, changing existing permissions or Free entitlements, billing, credential creation, deployment, or any later product expansion.

## Product contract

- Add one private Team Performance view inside the existing organization workspace. It applies equally to School and Club organizations.
- An authorized staff member selects one Team owned by the current organization (including an archived Team, clearly labelled) and one exact overs limit from 1 through 120.
- Analyze all matching recorded history: official, completed, limited-overs games with the selected format and exact overs limit. DLS-marked and shortened recorded innings remain eligible; no result or missing delivery is inferred.
- Display aggregate batting and bowling cards plus a coverage panel. Batting includes team and bat runs, wickets lost, legal balls/overs, run rate, legal dots, extras, and recorded fours/sixes. Bowling includes opposition team runs conceded, bowler-charged runs, team concession rate, bowling economy, team wickets, and bowler-credited wickets. Use aggregate ratios; undefined denominators display a dash. Eleven legal balls display as 1.5 overs.
- Team runs per wicket is not a player batting average. Do not expose player-level rows, identities, contact data, notes, deliveries, public projections, or exports.
- Always show: **“Based on recorded delivery data. Historical completeness has not been verified.”**

## Access and default-off authority

- Keep `school_free`/`club_free` entitlement records, capability sets, and their current resolver/API unchanged. `school_basic_statistics` is not authorization for this pilot.
- Add an independent `organization_team_performance` grant, default absent. A grant records its organization, validity start/end, granting operator, reason, and lifecycle status; revocation records its operator, time, and reason. Grant/revoke domain operations are idempotent and audit-ready. There is no public grant-management endpoint and no self-upgrade path.
- Do not seed, bootstrap, or create a production grant. Operational grant authority and actual pilot organizations remain owner decisions.
- On every read request, verify the active authenticated user, active organization, active organization membership, approved contextual role (`owner`, `admin`, or `coach`), current active grant and its validity, and exact Team ownership before calculation. Scorers and viewers are excluded. Personal subscriptions, `User.org_id`, creator/global roles, and superuser status never substitute for these checks.
- A user without active organization membership receives the existing non-disclosing organization 404. An authorized member with an unapproved role or no active pilot grant receives 403. An authorized member supplying a foreign/nonexistent Team receives a non-disclosing 404. A missing, future, expired, or revoked grant denies access.
- Revalidate membership, role, organization, grant, and Team for every request; client-side route hiding is not authorization.

## Analysis and response boundary

- Preserve the existing atomic malformed-game/innings exclusions, frozen-side and same-organization boundaries, dismissal validation, DLS/shortened-innings behavior, weighted aggregate rates, and 3/99-innings regression coverage.
- Keep ordinary cohort exclusions distinct from invalid/missing-data warnings. Coverage reports eligible/included game counts, usable batting/bowling innings, used rows, and distinct excluded game/innings counts. Never infer a completeness percentage; usable bowling does not imply usable batting.
- The API response is a typed allowlist of aggregate metrics, selected scope, and safe coverage only. Do not return source game IDs, innings attribution IDs, exclusion game/innings identifiers, raw deliveries, player identity/contact data, notes, public projections, or export links.
- Bound query/response workload. If the eligible cohort exceeds a configured hard limit, reject explicitly with a scope-too-large response; never silently truncate history.
- Distinguish unavailable access, loading, no eligible games, no usable ledger, usable data with exclusions, usable recorded data, request failure, and explicit scope-too-large states. Do not allow stale organization/team/format results to replace current state; clear protected data on organization/team/format changes, logout, or access loss.

## Explicit non-goals

No changes to scoring, DLS, results, personal plans, billing, existing Free statistics, public surfaces, or existing permissions. No phase names/calculations, recent-form analytics, coach reports, AI, exports, checkout, real-organization grant, trial lifecycle, or deployment in this slice. The owner-approved offer remains three further free premium games after readiness; this slice neither exposes nor consumes that offer.

## Issue #628 — locked metadata and readiness slice

### Focused audit and file boundary

The audit was performed against current `main` at `9db046bfd00da2a36c47b7264bbe277a30ef378c` (the post-#627 baseline). `Game` had no actual-play date; `Fixture.scheduled_date` is a plan, not evidence. Saved-Team setup freezes exact organization/Team identity and requested limits in `school_match_service.create_school_match`; `games.overs_limit` is subsequently mutable and cannot establish original format. Existing team analytics validates recorded-ledger identity and dismissal/run evidence, but filters on current overs and orders by Game ID, so neither that cohort nor ID order is a history/readiness source.

Allowed runtime changes are limited to `Game` metadata/audit persistence, its additive Alembic migration, the saved-Team match-setup capture point, the existing authenticated Game-mutation route/service boundary, a bounded internal readiness service and a read nested under the already gated Team Performance route. Tests and this spec/product checklist may change. Scoring, DLS, results, innings/practice rules, historical import, Selection, publication, entitlements/grants, ordinary statistics/Free APIs, trial/billing, player identity, public routes, and unrelated UI/workflows are protected.

### Metadata data contract and write authority

- `actual_played_date` is a nullable SQL `DATE`: the venue-local civil calendar date on which play occurred, with no inferred time or timezone conversion. It is independent of fixture schedule and creation/import timestamps. Only explicit staff confirmation makes it trusted.
- `original_overs_limit` is nullable and independent of current `overs_limit`. Capture it from the validated saved-Team limited-overs setup request before any later reduction. Do not infer it for existing games or from current limit/interruption data. Trust state/source distinguish unknown from setup capture and explicit correction.
- Every metadata change increments a per-game revision and writes old/new values, changed field, reason, provenance source, server timestamp, and actor to immutable audit history in the same transaction. The initial setup capture is audited. A confirmation/correction requires the caller's expected revision; stale writes fail without partial changes. Date-only confirmation never copies a Fixture schedule. Clearing a value is an audited correction back to unknown.
- A narrowly scoped authenticated metadata read/write reuses `require_game_mutation_access` inside the service, then independently requires unambiguous exact organization-owned saved-Team attribution. The read exposes current values/revision but no audit actors/history; the write does not add roles or require pilot/premium access. Ordinary scoring remains unchanged. The existing live overs-limit mutation continues to change only the effective limit and existing interruption record.

### Read contract, authority, and readiness rule

- The service is scoped to the active actor, current organization, exact saved Team ID, and original 25-over limited-overs format. School and Club have identical semantics. Any route must remain beneath the existing default-off Team Performance access check (active user, membership/contextual owner/admin/coach role, current grant, exact Team ownership); scorer/viewer and direct-service unauthorized calls fail closed.
- A counted game must be distinct, official, completed, limited-overs, not multi-day, have no unsupported custom rules, have trusted actual date and trusted original format equal to 25, and have exactly one matching frozen Team attribution with only a same-organization or external-opponent boundary. A genuinely external opponent has no `school_source` key; a present null/malformed source is unresolved, and every present saved-Team source must resolve to a Team in the same organization, including archived Teams. Practice, incomplete, wrong-format/current-limit-only, multi-day, and unsupported-rules rows are ordinary exclusions; unknown metadata, missing ledger, malformed/ambiguous ledger, and chronology problems are separately counted warnings/exclusions.
- Match-level ledger rule: the nonempty delivery list is atomic. Every row must be a dictionary with supported innings tag 1 or 2 and pass the existing strict frozen-side, participant, run/extras, and dismissal validator; both Team batting and Team bowling must be represented. Any malformed/ambiguous/unsupported row invalidates the entire game for readiness, even if other innings are usable. This is a conservative evidence rule, not a completeness/result claim; shorter/DLS innings do not get missing deliveries inferred.
- `as_of_date` is an explicit exclusive venue-local date boundary. An optional reference Game must be in the exact Team/organization scope, have trusted played date equal to that boundary, and is always excluded. A candidate dated on/after the boundary is not prior. A same-date group cannot establish internal prior order, so the whole group is excluded and counted as `same_date_ambiguous`; if such a group is as recent as or more recent than the fifth unambiguous usable date, return data-unavailable and no selection. Game ID may only make diagnostics stable, never claim chronology. Dates are ordered newest first; each Game counts once, and the latest five eligible dates form a stable internal selection.
- The safe readiness response contains only `required_history=5`, usable count, remaining count, ready/not-ready/data-unavailable state, stable fixed reason counts, and separate batting/bowling coverage counts. Selected Game IDs and metadata/ledger revisions are retained only inside the service result for later analytics; never serialize them, player identities, audit actors, raw ledger, or exclusion IDs through the aggregate pilot response. There is no completeness claim.
- Evaluate at most 250 candidate games and 5 MiB of candidate delivery JSON. Candidate Games and per-row payload lengths are fetched in one bounded SQL statement, preventing separate preflight and ORM hydration snapshots; `populate_existing` refreshes already-loaded Game rows so corrections in another session are observed. Saved-Team source IDs are validated in one bounded bulk query. Exceeding either cap returns explicit unavailable/scope-too-large; no truncation may produce a definitive readiness result. Candidate query order is deterministic but never substitutes for played-date chronology.

### Migration, rollback, and verification boundary

The migration adds nullable fact columns with conservative `unknown` defaults, trust/source consistency checks, revision default, an indexed per-Game audit table, and no historical data backfill. Clean downgrade removes the additive schema. Downgrade must refuse when any non-default metadata or audit history exists, rather than silently deleting corrections. PostgreSQL upgrade, downgrade on clean data, and clean re-upgrade are required in addition to SQLite tests; there is no production data operation.

Tests lock setup capture and limit preservation, unknown history, authorized/unauthorized direct writes, exact tenant ownership, atomic audited corrections and stale revisions, PostgreSQL conflicting-writer behavior, School/Club parity, 0/4/5/6+ usable games, latest-five and duplicate behavior, reference/future/date boundaries, tie exclusion, original 50→25 and 25→18 semantics, all exclusion classes, game-atomic invalid-ledger handling, safe response serialization, and query/payload overflow. Existing aggregate pilot, Free, match setup/Selection, scoring, DLS, results, and practice regressions remain required. Phase analysis, reports, and trial/offer behavior are later separately scoped work and remain undelivered.
